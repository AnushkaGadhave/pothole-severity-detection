Operational Boundaries & Scientific Disclaimer

CrackSight outputs are experimental computer-vision indicators. Severity tiers (Low, Medium, High) are provisional relative rankings calibrated strictly on the Pothole440 research partition. They do not represent certified municipal pavement urgency ratings (BMC) or geotechnical standards (IRC:82). Sensor depth maps are uncalibrated 8-bit integers [0, 190], not metric millimeters.

    CIVIL ENGINEERING & COMPUTER VISION PIPELINE

    Research Pipeline Specifications

    The exact architectural stages transforming road pavement imagery into observable geometric indicators. Click any stage to inspect its parameters.

    01
    Stage 1: Pavement Imagery Ingestion

    Standard 3-channel natural color RGB images [H, W, 3] captured from road survey cameras, optionally paired with 8-bit uncalibrated sensor depth maps.

    INPUT: RGB (uint8) OPTIONAL: Depth Array [0, 190] NORMALIZATION: 0.25 Scale Normalized Matrix
    02
    Stage 2: Spatial Feature Extraction

    Multi-scale color contrast, luminance gradient filters, and local patch variance are computed across the asphalt substrate to isolate pavement depressions.

    COLOR SPACE: Lab / YCrCb CONTRAST: Multi-scale Gaussian Derivatives TEXTURE: Local Variance Filter
    03
    Stage 3: 2D Segmentation Inference

    The trained classical multi-scale pixel classifier outputs pixel-level pothole cavity probabilities. Evaluated strictly on group-disjoint physical locations to prevent leakage.

    VALIDATION: Group-Disjoint 5-Fold METRICS: Mean Dice ~0.53 · Mean IoU ~0.39 DEVICE: CPU-Optimized Inference
    04
    Stage 4: Morphological Post-Processing

    Binary thresholding followed by 3×3 binary closing operations and internal hole-filling to consolidate disconnected cavity fragments and eliminate pixel noise.

    KERNEL: 3×3 Structuring Element CLOSING: Dilation then Erosion HOLE-FILL: Flood-Fill Interior Voids
    05
    Stage 5: 14 Planar Geometric Descriptors

    Extraction of two-dimensional region footprint metrics: mask area, coverage ratio, perimeter, circularity (4π·Area/P²), compactness, bounding box, aspect ratio, and axes.

    DESCRIPTOR COUNT: 14 Planar Features COORDINATES: Image Pixel Space (Uncalibrated) BOUNDS: Non-Metric
    06
    Stage 6: Provisional Relative Severity Formulation

    Relative Pothole Hazard Index (RPHI) computation using calibrated weights to assign provisional relative tiers (Low: 32.5%, Medium: 32.9%, High: 34.6%).

    FORMULA: RPHI = 0.40·I_rel + 0.35·A_mask + 0.25·V_proxy TERTILES: 33% / 66% Population Splits BOUNDS: Provisional Research Assessment

    Mathematical Formulation: Relative Pothole Hazard Index (RPHI)

    RPHI = 0.40 · Irel + 0.35 · Amask + 0.25 · Vproxy

    Where Irel is the maximum relative depression intensity, Amask is the normalized 2D footprint pixel area, and Vproxy is the integrated relative depression volume proxy. The coefficients were calibrated via 5-fold group-disjoint cross-validation on the 98 canonical physical locations to prevent spatial data leakage.
